About PRX

PRX is an open-source, local-first hobby project built for personal workout tracking. It doesn't track, sell, or collect your health data—everything stays on your device. Use it to track your gains, hit your PRs, and enjoy the process, but don't take it too seriously as professional fitness software! Stay safe and lift smart.
